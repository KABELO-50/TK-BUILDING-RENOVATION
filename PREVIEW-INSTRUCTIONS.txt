HOW TO PREVIEW BEFORE PUBLISHING

Option 1 (Windows):
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click PREVIEW-WEBSITE.bat.
3. The website will open in your default browser.

Option 2:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click index.html.

HOW TO PUBLISH ON NETLIFY
1. Sign in to Netlify.
2. Open Add new site > Deploy manually.
3. Drag this ZIP into the upload area.
4. Open the generated link and test Home, Gallery, Contact and WhatsApp.
